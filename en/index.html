About Us

Quality crafted,
batch by batch

In 2016, Anderson Santos and Reinaldo Moreira founded Kero Mais Pães Congelados in Goiânia. A love of baking and the desire to bring flavor and convenience to the table drive our factory every day.

Today we produce over 300,000 breads every day — with six product lines serving bakeries and food service in Goiás, the Brasília surroundings, Mato Grosso, Bahia, and Maranhão.
