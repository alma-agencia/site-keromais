Our origin

Started small,
grew with purpose

In 2016, Anderson Santos and Reinaldo Moreira founded Kero Mais Pães Congelados in Goiânia, turning into reality the dream of creating a quality frozen bread factory. They started with a single production line and a few partners who believed in the idea.

Over the decade, investment in fermentation, freezing at the perfect point, and consistent processes has led Kero+ to produce 30 tons a day — over 300,000 breads — for bakeries, retail, food service, and hospitality in Goiás, the Brasília surroundings, and Mato Grosso.

The mission remains simple and non-negotiable: reliability and excellence in every unit.

Our evolution

Ten years, milestone by milestone

  1. 2016

    Where it all began

    In Goiânia, Anderson Santos and Reinaldo Moreira found Kero Mais Pães Congelados, realizing the dream of creating a quality frozen bread factory.

  2. 2018

    The first partnerships

    Bakeries and chains in the region begin to rely on Kero+ frozen breads every day.

  3. 2020

    A growing portfolio

    The lines of breads, pão de queijo, sweet bakes, and savory snacks grow — more options for our customers' displays.

  4. 2022

    Arrival in Mato Grosso

    The branch in Rondonópolis (MT) brings Kero+ closer to new partners beyond Goiás.

  5. 2024

    Industrial scale

    Investment in frozen-food capacity and logistics accelerates production toward 30 tons a day.

  6. 2025

    Arrival in Bahia and Maranhão

    Kero+ expands into Bahia and reaches Balsas, in Maranhão — bringing the flavor of Goiás to new markets.

  7. 2026

    A decade of leadership

    10 years, 30 tons, and over 300,000 breads a day — with the same obsession with quality as on day one.
